ABOUT US

JALADURG is a dedicated platform to explore the fascinating sea forts of Maharashtra — Murud-Janjira, Sindhudurg, Vijaydurg, Kolaba, Suvarnadurg, and Madh Fort. These iconic forts, nestled along the scenic Konkan coastline, are a testament to the architectural brilliance and maritime history of ancient India. Each fort has a unique story, from the invincible Murud-Janjira to the strategic Sindhudurg, the historic Vijaydurg, the picturesque Kolaba, the golden Suvarnadurg, and the enduring Madh Fort. Designed for educational enrichment, historical exploration, and tourism guidance, our site offers in-depth insights into their construction, significance, and enduring legacy. Whether you're a history enthusiast, a student, or a traveler, we invite you to uncover the majestic legacy of these coastal gems and experience the blend of history, culture, and adventure they offer.
