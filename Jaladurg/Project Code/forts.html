Welcome to Murud-Janjira Fort

About the Fort

Murud-Janjira Fort is a majestic island fortress located in the Raigad district of Maharashtra. Built in the 15th century, this fort is renowned for its indomitable structure, which has stood resilient against numerous invasions. The fort's architecture blends Indian, Persian, and European styles, reflecting its diverse historical influences.

Highlights

The fort features 19 rounded bastions, several freshwater wells, and an impressive entrance that is visible only when close to the fort. The views of the Arabian Sea from the fort walls are breathtaking.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Murud, Raigad District, Maharashtra
  • Built By: Siddi Johar
  • Year: 15th Century
  • Unique Feature: Impregnable design
